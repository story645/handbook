# Year One: First Semester

The focus for the first year should be on clearing the requirements on the First Exam.

## First Semester

**Take CSC 70010 Algorithms**. The Algorithms course is offered every fall semester, and is a crucial component of the First Exam of the program.

Take additional courses, focus on the PhD Core Courses:

## Second Semester

Take CSC 80010 Research Survey. The Research Survey course is a mandatory component of the studies in Level I, and is usually offered every spring semester.

Take additional core courses to ensure you have the required total of 4 courses, including Algorithms, spread over all three categories.

Make sure you have a polished research tool on GitHub to demonstrate your software creation proficiency.

At this stage, the program office will ask for transfer from a previous Master's degree: up to 30 credits of relevant coursework may be transferred.

With a transfer, you should be able to reach 45 credits by the end of the first year, and proceed to Level II.

# Year Two

**With transfer credits:** continue to take courses to reach 60 credits, and if you do not already have an advisor, find one and figure out your intended area of research.

You should be able to finish Year Two with your Second Exam and progress to Level III.

**Without transfer credits:** continue to take courses to reach 45 credits. You should be able to proceed to Level II by the end of Year Two.

# Year Three

**With transfer credits:** do research, draw up your intended dissertation project as a project proposal.

**Without transfer credits:** take remaining courses to reach 60 credits. You may be able to finish the fall semester with your Second Exam and proceed to Level III in the spring. Then do research,  draw up your intended dissertation project as a project proposal.

Regardless of transfer status, sometime near the end of Year Three, beginning of Year Four, present your intended dissertation research to your Dissertation Proposal Committee.

# Year Four and Beyond

Present and defend your dissertation proposal.

Do your dissertation research.

Write your dissertation.

Present and defend your dissertation.

Submit your dissertation to the Graduate Center Library.

Graduate.

As many as possible of the required courses:

- CSc 74020 Machine Learning
- CSc 83060 Data Visualization

The remaining required courses, freely chosen electives, for a total of three courses. The Capstone internship or projects.
