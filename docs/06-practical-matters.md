# Program Affiliation

All publications (abstracts, posters, preprints, papers, patents) shall carry the appropriate one of the following Program Affiliations along with the appropriate campus affiliation: 
* Ph.D. Program in Computer Science, The Graduate Center of the City University of New York, New York, NY 10016
* M.Sc. Program in Data Science, The Graduate Center of the City University of New York, New York, NY 10016

# Time Off

You are entitled to all university holidays and any additional vacation time is the purview of your research advisor. Additionally, advisors must accommodate [religious observances](https://gc.catalog.cuny.edu/policies-of-the-graduate-center/religious-observances-student-rights). 

# Travel Abroad

Students who travel abroad for purposes related to their work at CUNY are required to purchase international travel insurance.  The paperwork must be submitted to the Office of the Vice President of Student Affairs at the Graduate Center at least two weeks prior to departure.  Details and forms can be found [here](https://www.gc.cuny.edu/student-affairs/international-travel-requirements-current-students).

# Departmental Communications

The Program routinely updates students on changes in departmental policies, research and funding opportunities and their progress to degree using their Graduate Center email address.  It is Graduate Center policy that personal email addresses not be used for official communications.   Failure to read your Graduate Center email may have serious consequences. 

# Mailing Addresses

Please inform the [APO](08-contacts.md) upon change of local address and telephone number while a student, and upon graduation. 

# Health Insurance

 Most department fellowships provide eligibility for low-cost health insurance through NYSHIP.  When students transition between CUNY campuses (Graduate Center to campus at the end of year one; campus A to campus B when they change advisors) their health insurance is not automatically transferred.  In addition, when students are placed on full grant support by their advisors and relieved of teaching duties, their health insurance is not automatically retained. Students must ensure that they still meet the employment [eligibility requirements](https://www.gc.cuny.edu/student-affairs/student-health-insurance-nyship) and should be proactive in ensuring that their NYSHIP coverage is continuous.  

# Transfer Credits

Students who have earned a master’s degree elsewhere may receive a maximum of 30 advanced standing transfer credits provided an official transcript and diploma is submitted with the application.  Please be advised, however, that transfer credits cannot be used toward the credit requirement of the campus-based master’s degree.
