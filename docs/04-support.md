---
title: Student Support
---

Students in the PhD Computer Science program are supported financially in one of the following ways:

* GCF - Graduate Center Fellowship. Provides tuition and stipend (at the rate of a Graduate Assistant A salary) for up to 5 years. The student is expected to contribute service to the program in year 1, teach at a CUNY College in years 2-4, and contribute service to undergraduate colleges through the *fifth year experience* in year 5.
* FSF - Full Science Fellowship (rare). Provides tuition for 5 years and stipend for the first 2 years. After that, responsibility for the stipend - as well as service commitments - falls on the advisor and the advisor's home college.
* HSF - Half Science Fellowship (most common). Provides tuition for 5 years and half the stipend for the first 2 years. Remaining stipend (i.e. half for 2 years, full stipend for 3 years) is the responsibility of the advisor and the adivsor's home college.
* TF - Tuition Fellowship. Provides only tuition support for 5 years. Stipend is the responsibility of the advisor and the adivsor's home college.
* CUNY Employee. In the rare cases that a CUNY lecturer chooses to pursue PhD studies, the tuition is covered by PSC/CUNY tuition waivers, and their employment is expected to cover the stipend sums.

After 5 years, the fellowship support terminates. A student who continues to be employed by a CUNY college can continue with the PSC/CUNY tuition waivers up to 2 additional years.

Students in the MSc and AdvCert programs in Data Science are responsible for their own financial support.
