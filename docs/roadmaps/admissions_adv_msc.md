::::{dropdown} Admission Requirements
Your application should show/demonstrate that you have: 

* A bachelor's degree (or equivalent) in Computer Science (or closely related field) from an accredited college or university
    * Non-STEM degree students may take courses from the NYU Tandon Bridge Program to substitute for degree and algorithms course requirements
* At least one course in  linear algebra, probability and statistics, and algorithms
* Fluency in programming in at least one of the following languages: Python, Java, or C++
* Minimum of 3.0 GPA in undergraduate or graduate course work 

:::{card}
In addition to the online application for admission, applicants must submit: 

* Statement of purpose and sample works
* 2 recommendation letters
* GRE Quantative Reasoning score of at least 80th percentile
* TOEFL or IELTS if post-secondary degree from an institution in which the language of instruction is **not** English-only
