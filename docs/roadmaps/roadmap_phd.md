# PhD Computer Science
F
## First Exam
	CSc 70010 Algorithms
	One of CSc 70010, 73010, 71010, 75100 (Algorithms and Theory)
	One of CSc 74011, 74020, 74030, 74040 (Artificial Intelligence)
	One of CSc 72010, 72020, 72030, 76010 (Systems and Computational Science)
	Total of four courses from the three categories above, Algorithms included, GPA at least 3.7.
## Level II
	First Exam passed
	CSc 80010 Research Survey
	45 Credits, out of which up to 30 may be transferred from a Master’s degree
## Level III
	At least two 8xxxx courses other than 80010, 80020
	60 credits, at least 30 in residence
	Written survey distributed to Second Exam Committee 4 weeks ahead of oral presentation date
	Survey presented to and approved by Second Exam Committee

## Dissertation
	Dissertation proposal distributed to Dissertation Proposal Committee 4 weeks ahead of oral presentation date
	Proposal presented to and approved by Dissertation Proposal Committee

## Defense
	At least one paper submitted for publication in either a peer-reviewed conference or a professional journal.
	Dissertation distributed to Examining Committee and Program Office 4 weeks ahead of oral presentation date
	Dissertation presented to and approved by Examining Committee
    Approved final version of dissertation deposited with the Mina Rees Library
