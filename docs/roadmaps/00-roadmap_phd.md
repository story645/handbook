::::::{card} Level II

- [ ] Select area of research and advisor
- [ ] Select Second Exam Committee: Advisor + 2 faculty members in CS or related interdisciplinary field
- [ ] Written (Second Exam) survey distributed to Second Exam Committee 4 weeks before oral presentation date
- [ ] Survey presented to and approved by Second Exam Committee
- [ ] Submit research tool to the executive officer
- [ ] At least two 3 credit 80000 level courses other than CSc 80010 Research Survey and CSc 80020 Computer Science research.
- [ ] 60 credits, at least 30 in residence
- [ ] No outstanding incompletes
- [ ] minimum cumulative GPA $\ge$ 3.00(B)

::::::{card} Level III
- [ ] Select dissertation proposal committee: Advisor + 2 faculty members in CS or related interdisciplinary field + 1 outside member (external of CUNY)
- [ ] Dissertation proposal distributed to Dissertation Proposal Committee 4 weeks ahead of oral presentation date
- [ ] Dissertation proposal presented to and approved by Dissertation Proposal Committee
- [ ] At least one paper submitted for publication in either a peer-reviewed conference or a professional journal.
- [ ] Dissertation distributed to Examining Committee and Program Office 4 weeks ahead of oral presentation date. 
- [ ] Dissertation presented to and approved by Examining Committee
- [ ] Approved final version of dissertation deposited with the Mina Rees Library
